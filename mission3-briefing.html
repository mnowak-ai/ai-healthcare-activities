01   Situation Report
Operations Intelligence Alert
Data Overload — Decision Bottleneck
Lakeside Clinic resolved its communication problems in Mission 2. But leadership is now facing a different kind of challenge — one that affects every decision made in this clinic every day.
"We now have too much data — but not enough clear decisions."
The clinic is collecting more operational data than ever before. Staffing reports. Scheduling systems. Patient flow metrics. Wait time dashboards. But no one can quickly turn that data into action. Bottlenecks are growing. Leadership is overwhelmed. That is why you have been called in.

02   Your Assignment
Active Role
Healthcare Operations Analyst
You are not a clinician. Your role is to interpret operational data, spot inefficiencies, and support leadership with clear, AI-assisted recommendations. You turn raw clinic data into decisions — and you know when to trust AI and when to override it.
Interpret Dashboards Spot Bottlenecks Use AI for Analysis Evaluate AI Output Recommend Actions
03   Mission Structure
Phase A
Data Interpretation
Read clinic dashboards and identify operational problems
Review real clinic dashboards: wait times, staffing levels, scheduling patterns, and patient flow
Identify where bottlenecks are forming and what data signals an operational problem
Recognize patterns in the data that affect clinic performance and patient experience
Document your observations before moving to AI-assisted analysis
Phase B
AI Decision Support
Use ChatGPT to analyze data and generate recommendations
Open ChatGPT — provided separately as an external tool
Use structured AI prompts to analyze the operational data you identified in Phase A
Ask AI to generate staffing and scheduling recommendations based on the data
Explore multiple AI-suggested options before forming your own opinion
Tool Required: ChatGPT — launch separately before starting this phase
Phase C
Human Judgment & Final Decision
Evaluate AI recommendations and justify your final operational plan
Review what the AI recommended — do not accept it without critical evaluation
Identify any incorrect assumptions, missing context, or risks in the AI output
Apply your own judgment to refine or correct the AI recommendations
Submit a final operational improvement plan with clear reasoning behind each decision
