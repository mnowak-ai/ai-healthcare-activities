Ethical decision-making is critical when using AI in healthcare environments.

⚠  Important — Read Before You Begin
In healthcare, using AI incorrectly can lead to privacy violations, biased decisions, or unsafe workflows that affect real patients. Your job in this mission is to evaluate risk carefully and make responsible AI decisions — not just use AI because it is available.

02   Mission Phases
Phase A
Risk Detection
Identify AI risks in healthcare scenarios
You will review realistic situations from Lakeside Clinic where AI is being used — some correctly, some incorrectly. Your job is to evaluate each scenario like a healthcare AI safety reviewer.
Identify privacy risks in how patient data is being used
Detect bias or unfair outcomes that could affect certain patient groups
Recognize unsafe or inappropriate AI use in clinical settings
Explain who could be harmed and how, in plain language
Output: AI Risk Identification Log
Phase B
Ethical AI Analysis
Use AI to analyze ethical impact — then evaluate it critically
You will use an AI tool — ChatGPT or similar — to analyze healthcare AI scenarios for ethical risks. This is not about what AI says is right. It is about learning to evaluate AI's suggestions critically.
Ask AI to identify ethical risks in a given healthcare scenario
Evaluate whether AI's suggestions are complete and correct
Identify bias, privacy, and safety impacts the AI may have missed
Decide where human judgment must override AI analysis
AI is a support tool in this phase — not the final authority. Your critical evaluation of AI output is the core skill being trained here.
Output: Ethical Impact Analysis Report
Phase C
Responsible Decision Making
Make safe AI decisions and design compliant workflows
You will act as a healthcare AI safety decision-maker for Lakeside Clinic. For each scenario, you will decide whether AI should be used, what protections are required, and how to design a workflow that keeps patients safe.
Decide whether AI should or should not be used in each scenario
Determine what safeguards and oversight are required
Design a responsible, compliant AI workflow for the clinic
Ensure patient safety, privacy, and fairness are protected
Output: Lakeside Responsible AI Safety Plan
💡 Key Idea for This Mission
In healthcare, the most important AI skill is not knowing what AI can do —
it is knowing when NOT to use it.
