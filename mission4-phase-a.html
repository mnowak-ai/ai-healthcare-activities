Phase A · Briefing

AI Risk Detection

You are acting as an AI Risk & Safety Analyst for Lakeside Community Health Clinic. Read four real scenarios from the clinic, identify what is risky about each one, and record your findings.

⚠ Important — Read Before You Begin

In healthcare, small mistakes with AI can lead to serious privacy violations, biased decisions, or unsafe patient workflows. Your job is to identify these risks before harm occurs.

There are no right or wrong answers here — this is about thinking carefully. Read each scenario slowly and consider: what could go wrong, and who could be hurt?

How This Phase Works
You will review 4 realistic healthcare AI scenarios
For each one, you will select the type of risk present
You will explain what makes it risky in your own words
You will identify who could be harmed and how
You will rate the severity of each risk
At the end, your findings become an AI Risk Identification Log
